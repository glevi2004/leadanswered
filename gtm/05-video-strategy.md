# LinkedIn video strategy

The thing new agency founders are crushing it on in 2026, almost entirely under-utilized because it's uncomfortable. Don't say "maybe" about this — either commit, or skip LinkedIn entirely.

## The 60–90 second video framework

Record a personalized video for each prospect. Structure:

**0:00–0:05 — Their name + something specific you noticed**
> "Hey Sarah — saw your med spa just launched the new Botox membership program, looks like you're scaling fast."

**0:05–0:35 — The hook: you tested their lead form**
> "I actually filled out your contact form 20 minutes ago using the name 'Mike Test.' Nobody's followed up yet. I bet that's not unusual."

**0:35–1:15 — The pitch + what you do (brief, concrete)**
> "What we do is text every new lead within 60 seconds — qualifies them, books the consult, syncs to your calendar. Same kind of thing your front desk does, but at 2am and on weekends."

**1:15–1:30 — The CTA**
> "15-minute call this week? Calendly link's in the message below this video."

## Production rules

- Phone, vertical orientation
- Decent lighting (window light is free)
- Walk while you talk if it helps energy
- **Do NOT over-produce.** Polished agency-style videos convert *worse* — they look templated. The point is that it's clearly *for them* and clearly human.

## Tools

- **Vidyard** — free tier has animated GIF previews → higher open rate on LinkedIn. Recommended.
- **Loom** — also works.

## Volume target

- **5–10 personalized videos/day, 5 days/week**
- ~100–200 videos per month

## Expected conversion (well-executed)

- ~30–40% will watch the video
- ~5–10% will reply
- ~2–3% will book a demo

So 100 videos/month → ~2–5 booked demos/month from LinkedIn alone.

Lower volume than calls — but the people who book from a video are way warmer. They self-selected after watching you for 90 seconds. **Close rates from video-sourced demos are typically 2x call-sourced.**
