# GTM strategy

Two channels, very different roles. Don't think of them as 50/50.

## Cold calls — volume + immediacy
Our primary pipeline driver. Generates same-day demos. **Most closed deals in months 1–3 come from here.**

## LinkedIn video outreach — quality + warmth
Lower volume, way higher conversion per touch. Generates fewer but better demos. Compounds over time as the profile builds credibility.

## How they stack

Calls produce immediate cash. LinkedIn video makes both the calls and the follow-ups land harder. Public LinkedIn content (added at week 7+) produces compounding inbound 6 months out. All three matter; **calls are the engine.**

## Channels in priority order

1. **Network.** Anyone we know who runs a business or knows business owners — friends' parents, professors, past internships, the GrantWare AI network from CS598. **The first 1–3 clients almost always come from here.**
2. **Personalized Loom / Vidyard videos** to specific prospects. Highest-converting outbound channel for AI agencies in 2026. See `05-video-strategy.md`.
3. **Local in-person.** Walk into med spas in Cambridge / Boston with a prepared demo on the laptop. Most agency founders refuse to do this — that's why they fail. The ones who do close 5–10x more deals.
4. **Vertical Facebook groups and forums.** Once we have a vertical, lurk in the groups. Don't pitch. Help. Inbound starts 2–3 months in.
5. **Cold email at scale** (Instantly, Smartlead) — only after we have case studies.

## What we deliberately don't do

- **Twitter / Instagram content for 6 months hoping clients show up.** Doesn't work for new agencies. Build an audience *after* you have revenue, not before.
- **Paid ads for our own agency.** ROI is terrible at this size. Money goes into tools that save delivery time instead.
- **SEO.** Six-month delay. We need cash in 60 days.
- **Boilerplate text DMs on LinkedIn.** Convert at <0.5% in 2026. Either go all-in on personalized video, or skip LinkedIn entirely.
- **Generic cold email at scale pre-proof.** Conversion is too low to justify the time before we have case studies.

## Sequence is everything

Calls + video + email aren't separate funnels. They're stacked into a 4-touch sequence — see `gtm/06-multi-touch-sequence.md`.
