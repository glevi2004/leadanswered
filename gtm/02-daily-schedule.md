# Daily schedule

100 dials + 5–8 personalized videos per day. Sustainable for 5 days/week. **Don't try to do 6** — burnout by week 4.

## Weekday block plan

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 8:30–9:00 | Wake, coffee, review yesterday's pipeline. **No Twitter, no Instagram.** Phone on DND except callbacks. |
| 9:00–11:30 | **Call block 1.** 50–60 dials. Best pickup window for SMBs. |
| 11:30–12:30 | Lunch + admin. Update CRM, send Calendly links to anyone who said "send me info," do callbacks. |
| 12:30–2:30 | **Call block 2.** 40–50 dials. |
| 2:30–4:00 | Demo calls if booked. Otherwise more dialing or follow-ups. |
| 4:00–5:30 | **LinkedIn video block.** 5–8 personalized videos for specific prospects. See `05-video-strategy.md`. |
| 5:30–7:00 | **Off.** Eat, gym, decompress. Non-negotiable. |
| 7:00–8:30 | Light prep. List building for tomorrow. Watch one of today's call recordings. Refine the pitch. |

## Why this shape

- Two call blocks > one big block — voice gets tired, energy dips after 90 minutes
- Videos at 4–5pm because the brain needs a different mode after dialing
- The 5:30–7 break is the most important thing on this page. Skip it for a week, then notice the call-block-2 numbers drop the week after.

## Non-negotiables

- 100 dials minimum *or* an honest accounting of why not
- One day per week off — no exceptions, no "I'll just do a half day"
- No outreach Sundays — reset day
