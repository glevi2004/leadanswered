# The 4-touch sequence

Don't run calls and LinkedIn as separate funnels. Stack them. Each touch reinforces the others.

## Cadence (per prospect)

| Day | Touch | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1 — morning** | Cold call | Phone |
| **Day 1 — evening** | Personalized LinkedIn video referencing the call | LinkedIn |
| **Day 3** | Call again. Mention the video if they answer. | Phone |
| **Day 7** | Email or LinkedIn message recapping. Soft CTA. | Email / LinkedIn |
| **Day 14** | Final call. Different angle (new pain point, industry news, anything fresh). | Phone |

## Sample LinkedIn video opener (after a missed call)

> "Hey Sarah, tried calling earlier — wanted to send this directly so you can see what I had in mind."

## Sample day-3 call opener (referencing the video)

> "Hey Sarah, Gabriel — sent you a quick LinkedIn video earlier this week about your lead follow-up. Did you get a chance to see it?"

## Why this converts higher than single-channel

We're not "calling and emailing the same prospect." We're orchestrating a multi-channel campaign where each touch builds context. By day 3 the prospect has heard the name 3+ times, seen the face once, and tested their own lead form alongside us. That's a fundamentally different state from a cold call.

## When to drop a prospect

- After day 14 with no engagement → archive, revisit in 60–90 days
- Hard "no thanks" → archive, don't argue, don't re-add
- Voicemail-only after 3 attempts → 1 LinkedIn video, then archive
