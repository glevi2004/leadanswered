# LinkedIn content plan (week 7+)

After ~30 days of personalized videos to prospects, layer in **public posting.** Not generic "AI is the future" garbage. Specific posts drawn from the actual work you're already doing.

## Why this is the long-tail moat

Cold calls produce immediate cash. Public LinkedIn content produces **compounding inbound 6 months from now.** Inbound leads close at 3–5x outbound. Two posts a week now → meaningful inbound by month 6.

This is content that's free because you're already doing the work.

## Cadence

**2 posts/week, max.** More than that and quality drops + the algorithm punishes you.

## Post formats that work

### The data observation
> "I called 50 med spas this week. 38 of them had broken lead follow-up. Here's what I saw."

Then 3–5 bullets of specific observations. End with one line of "what to do about it."

### The lead-form audit
> "Tested the lead forms on 20 Boston insurance agencies. 17 of them never responded. The 3 that did took 6+ hours."

Specific. Local. Numbers. No selling — let the implication land.

### The Loom recap
Short selfie-Loom (30–60s) walking through a common pattern. "Here's what I saw on the 40 med spa websites I checked this week."

## Post formats to avoid

- Inspirational quotes
- "Hot takes" on industry news with no original data
- Long humblebrag posts about how you almost gave up
- Anything that mentions "the entrepreneurship journey"

## Voice

Write the way you'd talk to a friend who runs a small business. Specific, occasionally funny, no jargon. Numbers over adjectives.
