# LinkedIn profile setup (do this BEFORE outreach)

When someone gets your video, they will click your profile within 30 seconds. If it says "CS Student at Boston University" with no business context, you're done — even if the video is great.

**Fix the profile before sending video #1. ~2–3 hours of work.**

## Minimum viable profile

### Headline
Clear, specific, no jargon. Examples:
- "Helping med spas convert more leads with AI-powered SMS follow-up"
- "I build AI text responders for service businesses — 60-second replies, 24/7"

Avoid: "Founder | AI | Entrepreneur" word-salad. State the offer.

### Banner
Simple. Free Canva template is fine. Tagline + brand colors. **Do not** put a stock photo of the New York skyline.

### About section
3–4 short paragraphs:
1. Who you help (one vertical or two)
2. What problem you solve (lead response in under 60 seconds)
3. Brief credibility (BU CS, GrantWare AI, technical background — framed for SMB owners, not engineers)
4. How to contact (Calendly + email)

### Featured section
Pin two things:
- A sample demo video (Loom or Vidyard — under 2 minutes)
- One case study — even if it's a self-built demo for a fictional client, be honest it's a demo

### Recent activity
Post 2–3 short pieces of content **before** starting outreach. Even just observations about lead follow-up. Empty profiles look like bots.

## Things that quietly kill conversion

- Profile picture in a hoodie or party photo → use a normal headshot
- "Open to work" banner → you're a founder, not a job seeker
- No employer listed → list "Answered (founder)" even when solo
- Wrong city → set Boston / Cambridge, local credibility matters
