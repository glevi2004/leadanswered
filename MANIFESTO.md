# Lu Computer — Manifesto

## The thesis

Every business runs on software it resents. A dozen dashboards — a CRM, an invoicing app, a scheduler,
a website builder, a help desk — each one a tool the owner has to log into and *operate*. The software
never does the work. It just gives you somewhere to do it yourself. You are still the operator.

**Lu Computer inverts that.** It is not another dashboard. It is a **computer for your business** — one
place where AI agents *do the work*, and you watch, steer, and approve.

## What it is

**Lu is the operating system for your company.** You talk to Lu the way you'd talk to a chief of staff:
"build my website," "chase the overdue invoices," "answer this customer." Lu doesn't hand you a form — it
delegates to a **department agent** that actually does the thing, live, on a **canvas** that is your
company's home.

Ask for a website and the **Engineering agent** spins up a real machine, writes real code with a real
coding agent, and ships a real site to a real URL — you watch it happen and hit Publish. Each department
— Engineering, Support, Finance, Sales, and the rest — is an agent with its own space, memory, and tools,
orbiting Lu at the center of the canvas.

## Why now

- **Agents can do real work.** They write software, hold conversations, and operate a computer — not just
  autocomplete. The frontier crossed from "assist" to "do."
- **Owners want the outcome, not the tool.** "A website," not "a website builder." "Paid," not "an
  invoicing app." "Handled," not "a help desk."
- **A business is a set of jobs to be done.** Give each job an agent, put them on one canvas under one
  orchestrator, and the software *is* the team.

## The principles

1. **Agents do, dashboards don't.** The unit of value is a job finished, not a screen to operate.
2. **One computer, one canvas.** Lu at the center, departments around it. You watch the work happen — you
   don't hunt across twelve tabs.
3. **Real outputs, real infrastructure.** The Engineer builds in a real sandbox and ships to a real URL.
   The terminal is a real machine. No mockups, no theater.
4. **You stay in command.** Agents propose; you approve what matters — publishing, spending, anything
   irreversible. Staged, legible, never a surprise.
5. **Boot with one, grow the company.** Onboarding boots your computer with the Engineer live; the other
   departments come online as they mature. The company assembles itself around you.

## Who it's for

Any business with work to be done and no wish to become a software operator. We start where the leverage
is clearest — owners who need a presence, a site, and the back office simply *run for them* — and broaden
as each department agent grows up.

## The bet

The company of the near future is not a team operating software. It is a founder and a computer full of
agents that do the work. **Lu Computer is that computer.**
