# The realistic 6–12 month picture

If we actually do the work — 4–6 hours/day of outreach, fast delivery, willing to talk to anyone — here's the realistic shape.

## Month 1 — bleeding
- 0–1 paying clients
- Setup, demos, getting the stack working
- Losing money on time invested
- **Goal:** ready to call by end of week 1, first paid pilot by end of week 4

## Months 2–3 — learning
- 2–4 clients
- ~$1,500–$3,000 MRR
- Pitch starting to feel natural
- **Goal:** write up a real case study from the first paying client

## Months 4–6 — traction
- 5–10 clients
- ~$4,000–$8,000 MRR + $3,000–$5,000 in setup fees
- Real income, still founder-led on everything
- **Goal:** known in one vertical, even informally

## Months 7–12 — leverage
- 12–20 clients
- ~$10,000–$20,000 MRR + ongoing setup fees
- Time to hire a VA for delivery, put more hours into sales
- **Goal:** referral pipeline generating 1+ qualified demo/week without outbound

## What this doesn't look like

The Twitter version where someone hits $50k MRR in 90 days is almost always:
- A lie
- A partner who's a sales monster (and they're not telling you that)
- A different business with much higher ACV they're rebranding

Boring. Compounding. 90 days of unsexy execution before the curve bends.
