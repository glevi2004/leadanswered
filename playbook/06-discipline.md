# The one thing that determines success

It's not the tech. Not the niche. Not the offer.

It's **whether we'll do uncomfortable outreach every day for 6 months before we see real money.**

## How agencies fail

Almost all fail at the same point: month 2–3. They've built the demo, sent some emails, gotten a few "interesteds" but no closes. So they decide "the offer isn't right" or "the niche is wrong" — when actually they just haven't done enough volume yet. They pivot, restart the clock, fail again.

The agencies that win are boring. They picked a niche, built a basic offer, did 50 outreach attempts a day for 90 days, and at some point a few clients said yes.

## The honest pre-commitment

Before starting any given day, answer:

> Will I do 100 calls / 5 LinkedIn videos *even on the days I'm tired, hungover, sad about a girl, or just don't feel like it?*

If yes: this works.

If no: scale the plan to match what we'll actually execute. **A consistent 50 calls/day beats an inconsistent 100.** Most people miss 1–2 days a week and don't realize they've cut their pipeline by 30–40%. By month 3 they're convinced "the strategy isn't working" when actually they ran it at 60% intensity.

## Mechanisms to enforce it

- Phone on do-not-disturb during call blocks
- Calls logged daily — non-negotiable metric
- A week with <70% of target = a real review, not just "I'll do better"
- One day off per week is sacred; two means we're slipping
