# Pricing

## First 3 clients — buy case studies, not revenue

For the first three deals we're trading margin for proof. Pick whichever the prospect responds to:

- **$500 setup + $300/mo**, or
- **$0 setup + $500/mo with a 6-month minimum**, or
- **Pay-on-performance: $50–$100 per booked appointment**

Lower than what the agency-Twitter crowd says? Yes — on purpose. We're buying case studies. Charge enough that the client takes it seriously, low enough that the decision is easy.

## Clients 4–10 — we have proof now

- **$1,500–$2,500 setup + $500–$1,000/mo**

Anchor on the ROI calculator on the landing page and on early client numbers.

## Clients 10+ — we're the obvious choice in the niche

- **$3,000–$5,000 setup + $1,000–$2,000/mo**

## Pricing traps to avoid

- **The "$10k setup, $2k/mo" Twitter agencies** usually (a) lie about their pricing, (b) sell to enterprise where it's justified, or (c) churn 50% of clients within 6 months. Don't anchor to them.
- **Discounting to close.** If a prospect won't pay $300/mo for the pilot, they're not ready for AI lead-response. They'll churn. Walk.
- **Performance-only past client 3.** Pay-per-booking is fine for the very first 1–2 to derisk; past that, MRR predictability matters more than upside.
