# Offering

## What we sell

Strip away the "AI" framing. We sell **speed-to-lead and consistency.**

The pitch in one sentence:
> When someone fills out your form or clicks your ad, our system texts them within 60 seconds, has a real conversation, qualifies them, and books them on your calendar. Even at 2am. Even when your sales rep is on another call. Even on weekends.

## Why it works

Harvard Business Review's lead-response study and a decade of follow-ups have shown leads contacted within 5 minutes are dramatically more likely to convert than leads contacted within 30 minutes. Most businesses know this and still can't execute on it. **That's our wedge.**

## What this is not

- Not a chatbot on a website.
- Not a generic "AI agency" trying to sell automations.
- Not a lead-gen service — we don't generate leads, we respond to them.

We answer inbound. That's the whole product.
