Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Lead Answered (“Lead Answered,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a lead-response service operated by Grovebox LLC. We act on behalf of the businesses (“Contractors”) that use our service to respond to people who contact them. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices — with particular attention to SMS/text messaging.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a contact or quote-request form on a Contractor’s website, we receive the information you provide, which typically includes:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Details about the project or service you’re requesting
  • Any other information you include in the form or in your text conversation with us

We also keep a record of the SMS messages exchanged so we can assist you and the Contractor.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Text you back on behalf of the Contractor about your request
  • Ask questions to understand and qualify your request
  • Propose and confirm appointment times
  • Notify the Contractor about your request and any booked appointment

Your mobile phone number is used to send you SMS text messages related to your service request.

3. SMS / Text Messaging

By submitting a Contractor’s contact form with your mobile number, you consent to receive SMS text messages from us (acting as the Contractor’s assistant) regarding your request.

  • Message frequency varies based on your conversation.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out and stop receiving texts.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at levi@leadanswered.com.

Data Sharing

  • Customer data is not shared with 3rd parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
  • Mobile opt-in and consent are never shared with anyone for any purpose. Any information sharing that may be mentioned elsewhere in this policy excludes mobile opt-in data.

Lead Answered Messaging Terms and Conditions

  1. The messaging program consists of general conversational messaging to answer questions, provide support, confirm service details, and schedule appointments for customers.
  2. You can cancel the SMS service at any time by texting STOP to the number from which you received messages. We will send a confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, after which you will receive no further messages. To rejoin, sign up again as you did initially.
  3. If you experience issues, reply HELP or email levi@leadanswered.com.
  4. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  5. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on communication needs. Contact your wireless provider with questions about your plan.
  6. For privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy above.

4. How We Share Information — and What We Never Share

We share the information you provide with the Contractor you contacted, so they can serve your request. We may also use service providers (such as our SMS provider) solely to operate the service on our behalf.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your phone number or personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Mobile opt-in and consent. No mobile information — including your phone number and your consent to receive text messages — will be shared with, sold to, or rented to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal or recordkeeping requirements, after which we delete or anonymize it.

6. Your Choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may also contact us at levi@leadanswered.com to ask about, correct, or request deletion of your information.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent version.
