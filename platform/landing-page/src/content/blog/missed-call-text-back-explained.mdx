> Answering your leads is one thing Sarah handles — one part of an operating system that runs the rest of your business by text, too. Here's the piece that catches the calls you miss.

Every missed call is a homeowner with a problem and their wallet already open. When you can't pick up, most of them don't leave a voicemail — they just call the next company on the list. **Missed-call text-back** is the fix: the moment a call goes unanswered, an automatic text goes out so the conversation keeps going instead of dying.

## How it works

1. Someone calls your business and you can't answer — you're on a ladder, driving, or it's after hours.
2. Within seconds, they get a text: *"Sorry we missed you! This is Apex Roofing — what can we help you with?"*
3. They reply by text (which feels easier than calling back), and the conversation picks up right where the call dropped.
4. From there you can answer questions, qualify the job, and book an estimate — all over text.

No new app for the homeowner. No voicemail tag. Just a reply fast enough to keep them from calling the next guy.

## Why it books jobs you're losing today

- **Most missed calls never call back.** A large share of first-time callers won't leave a voicemail and won't try again — they move on. A text turns that dead end into a live conversation.
- **Texts get read.** People ignore unknown calls but open texts within minutes. That's your foot in the door.
- **It covers nights and weekends.** Storms and emergencies don't happen 9-to-5, and neither do your competitors' auto-replies.

This is really the same idea as [the 5-minute window](/blog/the-5-minute-window): the first business to respond wins, and a text is the fastest way to respond.

## Text-back vs. a full assistant

A basic missed-call text-back just fires one canned message. That's better than silence — but the homeowner still has to be worked, qualified, and scheduled by a person. The bigger win is an assistant that handles the **whole** back-and-forth: answers their questions, checks the job is a fit, offers your real open slots, and drops a confirmed estimate on your calendar.
