"AI operating system" sounds like something a software company made up. Fair. Here's what it actually means for a contractor.

## The plain version

Right now, your business is spread across ten places: your phone for calls and texts, a form on your site, a CRM you half-use, a calendar, an invoicing app, Google for reviews, Facebook for posts, a notepad for everything else. Nothing talks to each other, and you're the glue.

An AI operating system replaces the glue. Instead of ten tabs, there's **one assistant you text** — Sarah — sitting on top of one app. You ask, she does it. That's the whole idea.

## What it covers

- **Get found** — your website, SEO (Google *and* AI answers like ChatGPT), blog posts from your jobs, social.
- **Win the work** — answer leads, draft quotes, book the schedule.
- **Get paid & grow** — invoices, reviews, follow-ups.
- **Run it all** — a CRM that works itself, your imported history, your team.

You do it by texting: "Quote the Miller job." "What's on Thursday?" "Text last year's customers for a review and write a blog post about the Miller job." One message, several things done.

## How it's different from a CRM

A CRM is a filing cabinet — it stores your customers and waits for you to do something with them. An operating system *does the work*. The CRM remembers the Millers; the operating system texts them, quotes them, books them, invoices them, and asks them for a review — because you told it to, in a sentence.
