Frequently asked.

Will it actually sound human? +

Yes. We're past the "Hi, I am a chatbot" era. Your AI is trained on hundreds of your ideal interactions and uses your tone, slang, and emojis. Most leads finish a conversation without realizing they weren't texting a human.

What happens when a lead asks something complicated? +

Two paths. If the question is in scope, the AI handles it. If it's outside (custom medical concern, legal question, complex pricing), it tells the lead a human will follow up shortly and pings your team in Slack/SMS with full context.

What if I already use a CRM? +

Perfect. We integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Follow Up Boss, and 30+ others. Contact info, transcript, qualification status, and the booked appointment all write back. Your reps live in their CRM.

How long until it's live? +

48 hours from your training call. Day 1: 30-min discovery to learn your services and qualification logic. Day 2: we ship a private demo for you to test. Day 3: you flip the switch on live leads.

What does it cost? +

A flat monthly fee smaller than the salary of one part-time receptionist — and it works around the clock. Exact pricing depends on lead volume and integrations. We'll quote you on the demo call.

Is this TCPA / HIPAA compliant? +

Yes. We only message leads who've opted in, every message includes opt-out language, and we sign BAAs for healthcare clients.
